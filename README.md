**Vice City Cover Studio** — turn any photo and caption into GTA-style multi-panel cover art, offline in the browser, exported as PNG. Pick one of five layouts (Vice Grid, Hero + Three, Full Bleed, Sunset Strip, Tilted Stack), drop a photo into each panel, drag to pan and scroll to zoom, choose one of five Vice City grades (Vice Sunset, Neon Night, Palm Gold, Ocean Drive, Noir Chrome), add halftone, film grain and a vignette, then set the Anton gradient title and Pacifico neon subtitle and export at 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080. No Rockstar assets anywhere; the Anton and Pacifico fonts are vendored under the SIL Open Font License.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Everything loads from `app/` — no network requests at all.

## Layouts & grades

Layouts: **Vice Grid** (2 × 2), **Hero + Three** (one big panel, three stacked), **Full Bleed** (a single photo edge to edge), **Sunset Strip** (three horizontal bands), **Tilted Stack** (three overlapping tilted cards). Grades: **Vice Sunset**, **Neon Night**, **Palm Gold**, **Ocean Drive**, **Noir Chrome** — each with halftone, film grain and vignette on top. Exports: 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 and 1920 × 1080 PNGs.
